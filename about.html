About NKOB CREATORS

NKOB Creators was founded in 2020 with a clear vision: combine creativity, technology and storytelling to build meaningful digital experiences. Today we are a distributed a good team , working with clients from Stockholm to Sydney, from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

Our approach is human‑centered: we listen first, then we prototype, then we refine. We believe technology should be beautiful, accessible and sustainable.

We're proud to have contributed to projects that raised funds for social causes, helped launch innovative products, and preserved cultural heritage. Our clients include NGOs, luxury retailers, museums, music labels and tech companies.

Beyond client work, we create personal projects: short films, generative art, and interactive installations. We also teach workshops and mentor young designers. In 2024 we launched the NOVA Fellowship, supporting five underrepresented artists.
