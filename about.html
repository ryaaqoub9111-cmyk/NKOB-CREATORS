About NKOB CREATORS

NKOB Creators was founded in 2020 with a clear vision: combine creativity, technology and storytelling to build meaningful digital experiences. Today we are a compact international studio delivering web, 3D visualization, video production and interactive works.

Our approach is human‑centered: we listen, prototype and refine. We believe technology should be beautiful, accessible and sustainable.

Beyond client work, we create personal projects — short films, generative art, and interactive installations. We teach workshops and mentor young designers.
